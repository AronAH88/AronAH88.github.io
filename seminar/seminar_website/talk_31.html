How to Do Functional Analysis in Infinity Categories

Speaker: Daming Zhou (SCMS)

Date: Monday, Sep 14, 2026

Time: 15:00 - 18:00 (with a 15 min break for tea in the middle)

Location: Room 1110, SIMIS

Abstract

In Part I, I will review functional analytic phenomena in condensed mathematics, including p-adic Banach spaces, Fréchet spaces, trace-class operators, and nuclear spaces, along with the definitions of dualizable categories and rigid categories. In Part II, I will explain Efimov's recent series of works using the perspective of functional analysis, and shows how this approach can be used to solve some problems Efimov left in his paper, I will also present an outlook toward a framework for noncommutative analytic geometry.
