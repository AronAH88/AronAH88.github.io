Enumerative Invariants from Calabi-Yau categories

Speaker: Junwu Tu (Shanghai Tech)

Date: Monday, Sep 09, 2024

Time: 15:00 - 18:00 (with a 15 min break for tea in the middle)

Location: TBD

Abstract

In the first half, we shall begin by recalling the fact that Hochschild cohomology of an associative algebra has a Gerstenhaber algebra structure. Then we discuss its chain level extension (conjectured by Deligne) that the Hochschild cochain complex carries an E2-algebra structure. Finally, we shall describe the construction of categorical enumerative invariants (CEI) in genus zero. In the second half, we discuss how to extend the previous genus zero construction to the higher genus setting, as well as the open string setting (work in progress).
