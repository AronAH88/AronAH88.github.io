Toward a theory of noncommutative del Pezzo surfaces via Artin--Schelter regular algebras

Speaker: Shinnosuke Okawa (Osaka University)

Date: Monday, May 11, 2026

Time: 26:00 - 05:00 (with a 15 min break for tea in the middle)

Location: Room 810, SIMIS

Abstract

The category of coherent sheaves on del Pezzo surfaces admit flat deformations which do not necessarily come from geometric deformations (a.k.a. commutative deformations). They should be regarded as categorical deformation quantizations of the Poisson structures on del Pezzo surfaces. For the projective plane and the quadric such deformations are obtained from certain classes of noncommutative algebras called Artin--Schelter regular quadratic/cubic Z-algebras. Moreover there is a 1-to-1 correspondence between such algebras and certain algebro-geometric data consisting of curves of genus 1 and line bundles on the curves. In this talk I will report on a joint project in progress (mainly with Kazushi Ueda) which aims to generalize this classical result to arbitrary del Pezzo surfaces. After explaining some background and reviewing the classical case, I will first explain how to define classes of Artin--Schelter regular algebras which yield noncommutative deformations of del Pezzo surfaces and how we recover the definitions of quadratic/cubic Z-algebra as special cases of our construction. I will then explain what we know so far and expect about the 1-to-1 correspondence between such algebras and algebro-geometric data. If time permits, I will also mention the conjectural action of affine Weyl groups on the set of isomorphism classes of AS-regular algebras.
