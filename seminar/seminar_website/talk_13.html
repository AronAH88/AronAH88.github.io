On the D-module of an isolated singularity

Speaker: Thomas Bitoun (University of Calgary)

Date: Wednesday, Feb 26, 2025

Time: 13:00 - 16:00 (with a 15 min break for tea in the middle)

Location: Room 1210, SIMIS

Abstract

Let Z be the germ of a complex hypersurface isolated singularity of equation f. We consider the family of analytic D-modules generated by the powers of 1/f and relate it to the pole order filtration on the top cohomology of the complement of {f=0}.
