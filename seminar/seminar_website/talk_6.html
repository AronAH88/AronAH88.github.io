Turaev Viro invariant from $\mathsf{U}_q\mathfrak{sl}(2;R)$

Speaker: Tianyue Liu (Peking University)

Date: Monday, May 26, 2025

Time: 16:00 - 19:00 (with a 15 min break for tea in the middle)

Location: Room 1110, SIMIS

Abstract

In this talk we discuss our recent work on defining a version of the Turaev Viro invariant for 3-manifolds using the positive representations of the quantum group $\mathsf{U}_q\mathfrak{sl}(2;R)$. This invariant has many nice properties, and we shall focus on an asymptotic behavior referred as the volume conjecture in the literature, which in our case holds for all hyperbolic 3-manifolds.
