Derived symmetries for crepant resolutions of hypersurfaces

Speaker: Will Donovan (YMSC, Tsinghua University)

Date: Monday, Nov 25, 2024

Time: 16:00 - 19:00 (with a 15 min break for tea in the middle)

Location: Room 1410, SIMIS

Abstract

Given a singularity with a crepant resolution, a symmetry of the derived category of coherent sheaves on the resolution may often be constructed using the formalism of spherical functors. I will introduce this, and new work (\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19555}{arXiv:2409.19555}) on general constructions of such symmetries for hypersurface singularities. This builds on previous results with Segal, and is inspired by work of Bodzenta-Bondal.
