SIMIS Seminar on Derived and Noncommutative Geometry：Moduli Stacks of Solutions to Non-linear PDES and their related Singularities

Speaker: Jacob Kryczka (BIMSA)

Date: Wednesday, Sep 18, 2024

Time: 16:00 - 19:00 (with a 15 min break for tea in the middle)

Location: Room 1410, SIMIS

Abstract

Many moduli spaces in geometry and physics, like those appearing in symplectic topology, quantum gauge field theory and in relation to homological mirror symmetry, are constructed as parametrizing spaces of solutions to nonlinear differential operators modulo symmetries of the underlying theory.
